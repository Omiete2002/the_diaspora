What’s On

Seven days. Seven moods. One home.

Every night at The Diaspora has its own identity, a predictable start time and an offer worth coming for. Monday to Wednesday are relaxed, play-along nights. Thursday to Saturday turn up the music. Sunday slows everything down.

Jenga, Uno, Scrabble, chess, Ludo, dominoes and group challenges, with relaxed Afrosoul and R&B in the background. Come alone and you’ll be dealt in.
