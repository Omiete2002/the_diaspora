Our Story

Many Cultures.
One Home.

A familiar meeting place for people who have lived everywhere.

Lagos, we’re coming home
LondonTorontoAtlantaHoustonJohannesburgDubaiLagos

Different journeys. One place to reconnect.

Wherever life has taken you — a degree in London, a career in Houston, a few years in Dubai — there is a moment when you land back in Lagos and want somewhere that feels like both. Somewhere you can walk in alone and leave with new friends. Somewhere the music, the drinks and the food speak every language you’ve picked up along the way.

That is The Diaspora: your new home for premium grills, signature cocktails, karaoke nights, games nights, after-work drinks, throwback music and unforgettable reunions, at 32 Musa Yar’Adua Street, Victoria Island.

How we do things

Simple, done properly

We keep the menu tight so every plate leaves the kitchen fast, consistent and generous.

Chicken, prawns, steak, salmon and burgers, flame-grilled and served on signature platter boards. A bar with international familiarity — the classics you love next to signatures named for the cities we’ve called home. Approachable service. A relaxed but stylish room. And something happening every single night.
