Welcome home

Lagos, we’re
coming home.

Different journeys. One place to reconnect. The Diaspora is a contemporary grill restaurant and social lounge in Victoria Island — for returning Nigerians, expatriates, internationally minded locals, professionals and visitors.

Arrive alone and comfortably meet people, or come with friends and enjoy a complete evening without needing to move elsewhere. Simple grills done properly, drinks from everywhere, a social calendar every night of the week, and service that makes you feel like a regular from day one.
