Welcome home

Lagos, we’re
coming home.

Different journeys. One place to reconnect. The Diaspora is a social lounge and grill in Victoria Island for returning Nigerians, expatriates, internationally minded locals, professionals and visitors.

Arrive alone and leave with new friends, or bring the whole crew and enjoy a complete evening without needing to move elsewhere. Good music, drinks from everywhere, something happening every night of the week, and service that makes you feel like a regular from day one.
